San Diego Gas & Electric (**SDG&E**) has some of the highest residential electricity rates in the continental United States. With summer peak rates frequently climbing above **$0.64 per kWh**, San Diego homeowners face high monthly utility bills.

Under **NEM 3.0**, traditional solar without battery storage is no longer enough. Sending daytime solar to the grid yields pennies ($0.05 - $0.08 / kWh), while evening electricity costs peak retail rates.

---

## Current SDG&E TOU-DR1 Rates (Summer 2026)

* **On-Peak (4:00 PM – 9:00 PM):** $0.62 – $0.68 / kWh
* **Off-Peak (6:00 AM – 4:00 PM):** $0.42 – $0.48 / kWh
* **Super Off-Peak (Midnight – 6:00 AM):** $0.24 – $0.28 / kWh

---

## Financial Payback Comparison: Solar Only vs. Solar + Battery

* **Solar Only System (8.5 kW):** ~$14,875 Net Investment | **9.0 Years Payback**
* **Solar + Battery System (8.5 kW + 13.5 kWh):** ~$20,550 Net Investment | **4.7 Years Payback**

## Step-by-Step Action Plan for San Diego Homeowners

1. **Battery Size:** Install 10 kWh to 15 kWh to cover the 4 PM - 9 PM peak block.
2. **Tax Credit:** Claim the 30% Federal Clean Energy Credit (IRS Form 5695).
3. **SGIP Rebates:** Check if your ZIP code qualifies for medical baseline or equity rebates.
