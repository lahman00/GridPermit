## Financial & ROI Breakdown

### 1. Tesla Powerwall 3: Best Value Per kWh
With 13.5 kWh of capacity and high peak output capable of starting whole-home HVAC units, Powerwall 3 provides the lowest dollar-per-kilowatt-hour cost. It is ideal for medium-to-large homes with high evening power demand.

### 2. Enphase IQ 5P: Best Modular Flexibility & Warranty
Enphase offers unmatched reliability with microinverter redundancy and a 15-year standard warranty. Its 5 kWh modular size allows homeowners with lower night consumption to start with a smaller capital outlay.
