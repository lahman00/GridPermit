# PG&E NEM 3.0 Solar Payback Calculator & Grid Interconnection Guide

If you are a PG&E customer looking to install solar panels or add battery storage, understanding **NEM 3.0 (Net Billing Tariff)** is critical to ensuring a positive return on investment.

## Key Changes Under NEM 3.0 for PG&E Customers
* **Export Credit Drop:** Solar energy sent back to the PG&E grid is now valued at avoided cost rates (~75% reduction compared to NEM 2.0).
* **Battery Storage Necessity:** Adding a solar battery (e.g., Tesla Powerwall) allows you to store excess daytime solar power and avoid expensive peak evening grid electricity.
* **Instant ROI Impact:** Systems without batteries now take **9–11 years** to pay off, while solar + battery systems pay off in **5–7 years**.
