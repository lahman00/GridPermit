# Release Safety
Require PASS evidence for tests, typecheck, build, SEO, partner health and release preservation. Production remains disallowed without explicit approval even when checks pass. Never click a live referral during verification. Record a rollback target and preserve unaffected URLs, routing, attribution, disclosure and geography.
