# Partner Operations
Read existing partner records and verified correspondence evidence. A partner is activation-ready only when status, commercial verification, approval reference, destination, territory and active tracking all pass. Keep missing items as blockers. Draft communications only; do not send, accept terms, create accounts, submit applications or expose personal data.
