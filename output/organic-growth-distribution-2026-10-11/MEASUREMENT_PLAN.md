# Measurement plan

Baseline date: **2026-10-11**  
GSC final-state baseline: **2026-09-11 through 2026-10-08**  
Reviews: **2026-10-18 (7d), 2026-10-25 (14d), 2026-11-08 (28d)**

## 7 days

- Confirm the three changed URLs build, index normally and retain correct canonicals.
- Record final-state GSC impressions, clicks, CTR and position when available.
- For a published LinkedIn action, record its activity URL, aggregate LinkedIn metrics and landing sessions with the campaign UTM.
- Keep content publication, landing sessions, CTA exposure, CTA clicks, outbound records and partner outcomes separate.

## 14 days

- Compare each page with its own baseline while showing position and available query mix alongside CTR.
- Check whether internal-link targets gain impressions or clicks; treat small movements as directional.
- Do not count reactions, comments or sessions as leads.

## 28 days

- Use a final-state 28-day window for the primary before/after observation.
- Report page deltas and available query rows, including privacy-threshold gaps.
- Reconcile an outbound record with a partner outcome only when an opaque CID and partner evidence match.
- Revenue stays UNKNOWN until received-cash evidence exists.

## Success criterion

A page is directionally improved if it gains a real organic click or sustains a CTR improvement without material position/query-mix deterioration. A distribution placement is useful if it produces attributable human landing sessions or substantive relevant discussion. Neither is commercial success until a real outbound referral and partner outcome are independently verified.
