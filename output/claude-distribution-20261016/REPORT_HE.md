California's remote inspection law, AB 1738, changes the city side of a solar project. It doesn't change the utility side.

Starting January 1, 2028, cities and counties have to offer remote inspections (live video, or recorded photos and video) for rooftop solar and storage on one- and two-family homes. Inspectors can still require an in-person visit. The SolarAPP Foundation says its virtual inspection tool has been piloting in five California jurisdictions since August and should be generally available in early 2027.

That could shorten the wait for a final local inspection. What comes after stays the same: the utility's interconnection review, any meter work, and written Permission to Operate. Those follow the utility's process, not the city's.

If you're tracking a project, keep two dates: when the city signed off, and when the utility granted PTO. Here's how the two tracks fit together:
