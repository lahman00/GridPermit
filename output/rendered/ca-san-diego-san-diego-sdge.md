## Generation Supplier

**Name:** San Diego Community Power (SDCP)
**Type:** cca

*Value notes:* The City of San Diego is a founding member/customer of San Diego Community Power, a joint-powers Community Choice Aggregator that purchases/generates electricity on customers' behalf. SDG&E remains the transmission/distribution entity and delivers the electricity SDCP procures. # Battery Programs

- **SGIP - Residential Solar and Storage Equity** — administered by Center for Sustainable Energy (CSE), on behalf of SDG&E, under the CPUC's Self-Generation Incentive Program
  Storage incentive of $1,100/kWh paired with a solar incentive of $3,100/kW, funded via a $280 million CPUC-authorized statewide budget that opened for reservations June 2, 2025. This is the same statewide SGIP tier used in the PG&E-territory pilot records; SDG&E-territory administration is confirmed to be handled by CSE rather than SDG&E directly.
  Value: $1100/kWh
  Eligibility: PG&E, SCE, or SDG&E residential customers (general market tier)
  Link: https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/industries-and-topics/electrical-energy/demand-side-management/self-generation-incentive-program
  Status: unknown (from 2025-06-02)
- **SGIP - Equity Resiliency** — administered by Center for Sustainable Energy (CSE), on behalf of SDG&E, under the CPUC's Self-Generation Incentive Program
  Incentive of $1,000/kWh for IOU residential or non-residential customers. Source states this rate is/was 'available through 2025.'
  Value: $1000/kWh
  Eligibility: IOU residential or non-residential customers meeting Equity Resiliency criteria (e.g. high fire-threat district, medical baseline). Specific San Diego eligibility not independently confirmed this session.
  Link: https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/industries-and-topics/electrical-energy/demand-side-management/self-generation-incentive-program
  Status: expired (until 2025-12-31)
