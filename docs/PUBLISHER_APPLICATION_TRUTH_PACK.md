# GridPermit Publisher Application Truth Pack

Last reconciled: 2026-08-26

## Purpose

Use this pack when an affiliate network, advertiser, CPL network or pay-per-call program asks for a publisher description, audience, traffic source, promotional methods or intended placements.

It exists to make authenticated browser applications faster while preventing invented traffic, revenue, business, performance, identity or compliance claims.

This is not a substitute for reviewing live terms. Tax, identity, payment, legal-entity and binding acceptance fields remain owner-controlled.

Our primary acquisition channel is organic search. We use contextual links only within relevant informational content and do not use incentivized traffic, purchased lists, outbound calling, transferred calls, fabricated leads or misleading claims. GridPermit is operated by a non-U.S. publisher located in Israel and serves primarily U.S. readers.

We will not bid on protected brand terms or use coupon, cashback, email, SMS or paid-media promotion unless separately approved in writing.

## Terms review checklist

Before any `Apply`, `Join`, `Accept`, signature or certification:

1. advertiser identity and program ID
2. payable action and exact commission/payout
3. attribution/cookie window
4. allowed traffic sources
5. protected trademark and paid-search restrictions
6. coupon, cashback and promotional-code rules
7. geography and product exclusions
8. returns, reversals, lock period and clawbacks
9. creative approval and disclosure rules
10. privacy, consent, TCPA/TSR or call-recording obligations
11. payment cadence, threshold and method
12. tax and identity requirements
13. exclusivity, minimum volume, insurance, audit, indemnity and termination terms
14. fees, deposits or paid subscriptions

Stop for owner review when a live term introduces an unexpected material obligation or conflicts with the verified commercial baseline.

## After submission

Record:

- exact network and advertiser name
- advertiser/program ID
- account/publisher name used
- date and observable submission confirmation
- live terms captured
- pending, approved or rejected status
- issued tracking link, deep link or phone number
- required disclosure and placement restrictions

Never enable production from an application confirmation alone. Approval, real attributable tracking and deliberate launch gating are all required.
