Reusable, personalized outreach templates for the four contact types GridPermit uses. No em dash. No traffic-volume claims. No "large audience" language. No invented conversion potential. No mention of Israel unless a form's own eligibility/KYC step specifically requires a tax residency answer.

Fill in `{Brand}`, `{contact_name}` (if known), and `{specific_ask}` before sending. Keep every fact consistent with `docs/PUBLISHER_APPLICATION_PACKET.md`.

## Rules for every outreach

- Never state a traffic figure, conversion estimate, or revenue projection.
- Never say "large audience," "significant traffic," or similar unverifiable claims.
- Never mention Israel, tax residency, or KYC details unless the partner's own application form explicitly requires that field.
- Never accept a contract, exclusivity, deposit, or payment commitment via email; always defer those to the owner.
- Label outreach consistently (e.g. a mail label or subject prefix) so replies are easy to find and pipeline status stays accurate.
