## Source decisions

The [code page](https://longmontcolorado.gov/building-inspection/building-codes/) lists newer editions while announcing a NEC 2026 process whose final effective date was not established. The document list and its FAQ/schema answer keep this qualification. Old numerical design loads are not copied into the guide.
- The solar checklist's licensed-electrical-contractor rule is not overridden by the general homeowner provision. A solar DIY exception remains unverified.
- The current fee manual gives a general valuation/review/tax framework. A complete solar price and current statutory cap were not established; amounts remain null. The old HB11-1199 PDF linked by the City is not evidence of the cap in force today.
- [LPC's current solar page](https://longmontcolorado.gov/longmont-power-communications/renewable-energy/solar-energy/) and [interconnection checklist](https://longmontcolorado.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Interconnection-Application-Checklist.pdf) distinguish city/county permit submissions, utility review, inspection and permission to operate. Utility territory and city limits are separate checks. The `-multi` record guard does not establish a new paid-referral route.
- LPC's wholesale supply from Platte River is sourced; no battery incentive or solar installation rebate is invented. New/expanded-system export-credit terms are qualified, with no savings estimate.
