## Source decisions

The record cites 15 official City, County and utility sources. Municipal claims were independently checked against the City's current pages. Xcel's four program/interconnection pages use the browser captures already verified on November 14; their account restrictions remain explicit.

- The [June 2026 process change](https://www.arvadaco.gov/1545/2026-Process-Improvements) removes formal pre-issuance building plan review for residential solar and residential storage. It does not establish an engineering exemption, instant issuance or exemption from permits and inspections.
- The [2026 fee schedule](https://www.arvadaco.gov/1263/Building-Fee-Schedule) lists separate $55 solar PV and residential ESS entries. They are not a total project price or an instruction to add two permit charges. Use tax and project-specific charges require confirmation.
- The [posted Solar checklist](https://www.arvadaco.gov/522/Solar) retains older fees, timing and code references. Its document list is qualified before the checklist and in the visible FAQ/FAQPage answer. The June change does not specify which attachments can be omitted.
- The [adopted-code list](https://www.arvadaco.gov/165/Adopted-building-codes-and-design-criter) includes NEC 2026; the [transition page](https://www.arvadaco.gov/1446/For-Builders-Detailed-changes-coming-in-) still retains a NEC 2023 transition note. The guide discloses the unresolved conflict and does not choose an operative electrical edition for the reader.
- [Building Permits](https://www.arvadaco.gov/332/Building-Permits) provides general three-business-day guidance for complete applications without plan review. A solar-specific numerical timeline remains null. Applying as a homeowner is not treated as unrestricted DIY electrical authorization.
- The City names Xcel, while [United Power lists Arvada](https://www.unitedpower.com/service-area). The record uses the `-multi` utility guard and gives both official interconnection links. Xcel incentives are expressly limited to Xcel accounts; [United Power reports no rebate for new installations](https://www.unitedpower.com/connect-your-solar).
- Jefferson and Adams counties both list Arvada. The City's address-verification tool is the starting point for permit jurisdiction; no private address or resident permit file was queried.

Automated City URL checks return HTTP 403; the material was separately opened using web tools. These warnings are retained rather than reported as clean automated link checks.
