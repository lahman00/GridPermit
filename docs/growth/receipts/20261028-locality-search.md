# Locality search clarity, 2026-10-28

Scope: existing Bakersfield, Fresno and Oxnard guides only. Search evidence is
stored privately, outside this public repository. Chula Vista and the other
existing experiments are unchanged. No new page, payment path or paid route.

## Official evidence reviewed

- Bakersfield: [City PV Toolkit, Document 1](https://content.civicplus.com/api/assets/ca-bakersfield/a531a051-d462-4974-ac9d-d8eb4ac9067a?cache=1800), sections 2 and 5 support the checklist, plans and inspection wording. No fee or turnaround is promoted in the new copy. The old toolkit is not treated as proof that newer pathways are unavailable.
- Oxnard: [City online solar instructions](https://sites.google.com/oxnard.org/onlinesolarpermitswithsolarapp/home) explicitly restrict this SolarAPP+/Click2Gov route to licensed contractors and list the City Business Tax Certificate and inspection step. No homeowner pathway, fee or size limit was inferred.
- Fresno City: [SolarAPP+ instructions](https://www.fresno.gov/planning/get-an-instantly-approved-solar-permit-through-solar-app/) and [Building and Safety](https://www.fresno.gov/planning/building-and-safety/) support the City Accela handoff and application search/status links.
- Fresno County: [County SolarAPP+ instructions](https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/Departments/Public-Works-and-Planning/divisions-of-public-works-and-planning/development-services-division/building-safety/SolarAPP-Permits) distinguish the County-issued permit from SolarAPP+ confirmation and link the County citizens portal. That portal's contents were blocked from direct retrieval; only its role as the County's linked destination is stated.

All four sources above were read on 2026-10-28. This review covers the changed
copy only; locality-wide verification dates, fees and underlying records remain
unchanged. GridPermit is identified as independent of the permit offices.
