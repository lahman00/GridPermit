# EXP-03: title and description proposals (preview only, nothing deployed)

Baseline impressions, clicks and CTR for each page are in a private file outside the repo. This note has no Search Console numbers.

## Which pages qualify
The brief asked for pages at positions 4 to 10 with at least 30 impressions. In the latest authenticated export (2026-09-10..2026-10-08) six pages qualify. Four are locked by running measurements (the PTO link pilot, a reviewed permit-path block) and must not change. Two are free: `/blog/sdge-guide/` and the Sacramento guide.

## Honest limit: this cannot be a measured test
Those two pages had a few dozen to under a hundred impressions in 28 days and no clicks. At those volumes, a title change would move expected clicks by well under one, so no 28-day window can show a win or a loss. The success rule in the brief therefore cannot be evaluated for this sample. Treat the drafts as a free, low-risk wording improvement, judged by reading, not as an experiment. A real test needs pages with a few hundred impressions per month or a pooled site-wide change.
