## The offer to test: a prepaid, manual research pilot (concierge test)
For small California installers that do residential PV plus battery work.
- You send up to three upcoming jobs (jurisdiction, utility, system type, battery yes/no).
- For each job GridPermit returns a short brief within a stated turnaround: who issues the permit, what the official page says about documents, inspections and fees, the separate utility interconnection and PTO steps, and everything that is NOT verified, each with source URL and date.
- Full refund if the briefs are not useful. No software, no account, no integration, no filing on your behalf, and no legal or engineering advice.
