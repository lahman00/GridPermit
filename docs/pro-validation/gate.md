# Validation gate (14 days, no code)

Owner approval 2026-10-24: approved as documents for the pilot. Not started. Outreach, the payment method and the first contact each still need separate owner approval; the 14-day window starts at the first approved contact. Nothing is published or sent.
Fixed before any outreach so the result cannot be argued afterwards.

- **Window:** 14 days from the first approved contact.
- **No product code** is written, no accounts or billing are built, no public page claims the product exists.
- **Count only real events:** a conversation is a completed interview; a prepaid pilot is money received (or an invoice paid), not a verbal yes.
- **GO:** at least 3 prepaid pilots from distinct companies.
- **REVISE:** 1 or 2 prepaid pilots, or several interviews with a dated, costly problem but no payment. Change the offer, the audience or the price hypothesis once and run one more 14-day window.
- **STOP:** 0 payments after 12 conversations. Do not build Pro; keep the guides, the Agensi products and partner referrals as the revenue paths.
- **Evidence to keep (outside the public repo):** interview notes, the payment record, the reasons for every no.
- **Before outreach:** owner approves the offer text, the list and the payment method. Outreach, refunds and any commitment are the owner's actions.
