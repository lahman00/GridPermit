# Building the installer list (public sources only)

Purpose: a named list of California residential installers to invite to the pilot in `offer.md`. Building the list is research only. Do not contact anyone until the owner approves the list and the message.

## Sources allowed
- The California Contractors State License Board public license lookup (confirm an active C-10 electrical or C-46 solar classification and the business name).
- The company's own website, public Google Business profile and public project pages.
- Public permit or SolarAPP+ participation pages that name licensed contractors, where a city publishes them.
Not allowed: scraping personal emails, buying lists, social-media DMs, any non-public directory.

## Fit criteria
1. Active California license with a solar or electrical classification.
2. Installs residential PV, and shows battery or storage work on its own site (prefer these).
3. Works in more than one city or county (the problem GridPermit solves is repeat jurisdiction research).
4. Small enough that permit research is done by the owner or a coordinator: a handful of staff, not a national installer with a permitting department.
5. A published business contact (a contact form or business email on its site).

## Record per company (keep outside the public repo)
Name, license number and classification, cities served, battery work yes/no with the page URL as evidence, business contact route and its URL, date checked, status (CANDIDATE, APPROVED_FOR_OUTREACH, CONTACTED, REPLIED, DECLINED). Never add personal data about individuals.

## Size and quality
Target 30 candidates to reach 12 conversations; adjust after the first batch of replies. Note any bias in who you can find (companies with good websites).
