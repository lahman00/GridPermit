# Installer interview script (15 to 20 minutes)

Rules: ask about the past, not hypotheticals; do not pitch before the questions; do not promise features, results or approvals; record answers in the interviewee's words; ask for permission before taking notes. Do not collect customer data or ask for project documents.

## Opening
"I'm researching how installers handle permit and utility steps. No sales pitch for the first part. May I take notes?"

## The last delay
1. Tell me about the last time a permit, a fee, an inspection or interconnection/PTO held up a project. What happened, which city and utility?
2. How many days did it cost, and what did the delay cost you or the customer?
3. What was the first thing you did to find the requirement? Which sources did you trust? Were any wrong or out of date?
4. Who does that research (you, a coordinator, an outside service)? How many hours a week?
5. How often do you work in a city you have not permitted in before?

## What you use and pay today
6. What tools, services or consultants do you use for permit packages, plan sets or tracking? What do you pay for each, monthly or per job?
7. What have you tried and stopped using, and why?
8. For battery or storage jobs, what is different about permit or PTO steps for you?

## Test the idea (only after the questions)
9. If a source-linked brief for an upcoming job arrived with the official pages, dates and a list of what is NOT verified, what would you do with it? What would make you not trust it?
10. What would a useful brief have to contain for you to pay for it? What would you not pay for?

## The payment ask
11. I'm running a small prepaid pilot: three briefs for upcoming jobs, full refund if they are not useful. Would you like to start? (Record yes, no, later, and the reason, in their words.)
12. If not now, what would have to be true?

## Close
Thank them. Do not follow up unless they said yes or asked you to.

## Scoring (record, do not interpret in the room)
Problem recent and costly (a dated example), pays today for something similar, agreed to the prepaid pilot, reason for any no.
