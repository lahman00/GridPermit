# Awin Cluster — Application Kit

Written 2026-08-20, expanded 2026-08-21. Complete, ready-to-execute record for all 6 Awin-network candidates so the entire cluster can be applied to same-day the moment the Awin publisher account exists — no new investigation needed at that point. All positioning copy is built from `docs/PARTNER_APPLICATION_PROFILE.md`'s verified facts; nothing here estimates traffic, followers, or social presence. Merchant IDs and commission/cookie figures below are primary-sourced (Awin's own merchant-profile pages) — re-confirmed 2026-08-20/21, not re-fetched further to avoid redundant research.

## Step 0 — the one owner action this whole kit depends on

`ui.awin.com/publisher-signup` → Account Setup (Company Name, Tax Residency, First/Last Name, Email, **Password**) → a real, refundable **~£5–6 card deposit** → Promotional Type → Promotional Space → Verification. Account-creation + payment — owner-only. Everything below assumes this step is done. **Awin's publisher-account approval itself is typically processed within ~24 hours** (per Awin's own compliance documentation, confirmed in an earlier pass) — the per-program joins below are a separate, per-advertiser decision on top of that.

## Awin's own "Promotional Space" step (asked once, for the account — not per-program)

> GridPermit (mygridpermit.com) publishes source-verified solar and battery permit guides for US homeowners — 341 city guides across 44 states, each fact tied to a cited official government or utility source. Promotion is organic search and on-page contextual placement only — no paid traffic, no email list, no social media presence, no incentivized or co-registration traffic.

**Website field:** `https://mygridpermit.com`
**Promotional methods to select:** Content/Editorial, Organic Search, Comparison/Review content
**Promotional methods to leave unchecked:** Paid Search, Email, Social Media, Coupon/Deal Sites, Incentivized Traffic

## Application sequence once the Awin account exists

1. Complete Awin's own Promotional Space step (copy above).
2. Apply to all 6 merchants in the same session — each is an independent "Apply" click inside the Awin dashboard, no cross-dependency between them.
3. Recommended order (by strength of terms/fit, not a technical requirement): Bark.com → Power Queen → ALLPOWERS → Bluetti → MatchBurst → EcoFlow.
4. Record each result (approved/pending/rejected) in `docs/AFFILIATE_PARTNER_PIPELINE.md`'s master index the same day.

- Does not invent traffic numbers, follower counts, a legal entity name, or an "approval type" where none was published — those gaps are marked "not explicitly published," not guessed.
- Does not claim any of these six are approved — this is preparation only.
