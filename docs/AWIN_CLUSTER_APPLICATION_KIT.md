Placement would be contextual, on relevant guide pages, not a dedicated Bark-specific page.

### 2. Power Queen (Awin merchant 118441 — 5.5% base, 30-day cookie, solar explicitly named)
> GridPermit publishes 341 solar/battery permit guides for US homeowners. Power Queen's battery/power-station products are a direct content match for readers researching battery backup permitting — placement would sit alongside our existing battery-related guide content.

### 3. MatchBurst (Awin merchant 114854 — CPL, US-only traffic, solar is a named vertical)

### 4. Bluetti (up to 10% commission, 30-day cookie, network choice includes Awin)
> GridPermit's battery/backup-power permit guides are a direct content match for Bluetti's home-battery product line. We'd feature Bluetti as a recommended-equipment reference for homeowners researching battery installation permitting.

### 5. EcoFlow (minimum 5% commission, 7-day cookie)
