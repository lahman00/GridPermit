# SDG&E Solar Billing Plan guide: source review, November 6, 2026

Page: `/blog/sdge-guide/`

## Reader task and bounded change

Help a residential homeowner identify the account-specific inputs needed to evaluate a solar or battery proposal. The existing guide now links directly to official rate, export-pricing and usage-data resources and distinguishes CCA generation from SDG&E delivery. It corrects the old generic fixed-charge/minimum-bill wording and distinguishes grid flows from full solar production.

Preserve the URL and original publication date. Set `updatedDate` to the actual substantive edit date. The title/description will also propagate through existing generated article links and search cards. No new city, permit or tariff eligibility claims, price table, savings forecast or calculator is introduced.

## Verification limits and commercial preservation

Sources were reviewed November 6, 2026. SDG&E direct browser/HTTP access was blocked; its official indexed text and document extracts were used through the web reader. CPUC was also observed in the rendered browser. No utility customer account, bill or interval file was accessed. The guide is not a live rate feed.

An independent read-only source/copy reviewer found no material blocker. Ben-adam was applied in embedded English editorial mode with a second pass: no unsupported facts, personal history, guarantees or urgency introduced. Battery comparison questions, conservative scenarios and avoiding double-counted energy are analytical checks, not invented utility requirements.

The City of San Diego/SDG&E/existing-solar paid-referral paragraph and its exact destination are preserved verbatim, located in the battery section. No geographic or partner eligibility is broadened. The $9 organizer is an optional blank workbook with existing quote-comparison/action/source-check templates; it is explicitly not a calculator, savings model or filing service. Checkout, price, downloads and refund terms are unchanged.

Private search baselines, release identifiers, QA and rollback evidence remain outside the public repository. Publication is not evidence of Google recrawl, ranking improvement, traffic or sales.
