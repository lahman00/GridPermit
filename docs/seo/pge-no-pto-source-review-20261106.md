| [PG&E: Interconnection Checklist (PDF)](https://www.pge.com/assets/pge/docs/about/doing-business-with-pge/interconnection-checklist.pdf) | Project/account identifiers, equipment details and final certificate address, work scope, approval date and signature. | The checklist is labeled Standard NEM.
